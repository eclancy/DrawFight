# DoomBot's story - PROTOCOL: CLEAN FRIDGE - and the finale every other story ends with.
#
# How a story script reads (the whole language - .ai/story-mode.md has the rest):
#
#   == chapters                     the story, in order, one chapter a line (see below)
#   == opening                      a scene, by name, down to the next == heading
#   doombot: HELLO.                 someone says something, in a speech bubble
#   doombot (shout): MINE.          ...said a certain way: shout, whisper, scared, happy, hot, slam
#   doombot: [fridge] [=] [doombot] a picture in the bubble (heart, fridge, doombot, =, ?, !, star,
#                                   magnet, bomb, flame, nofire, square) - nobody uses one now
#   narrator: One by one...         the narrator, on a strip of paper along the bottom
#   narrator (shout): IF THAT...    the grown-up in the kitchen
#   doombot writes: 1. FRIDGE.      burned onto the fridge door with his eye laser (see "board")
#
#   player                          whoever's story it is; anyone else by name (flambe, lugnut,
#                                   edgelord, circy, triguy, doombot, and mk2 - DoomBot Mk. II)
#   cast player at -400             where someone stands when the scene starts (on the solid
#                                   ground there); add "facing left" or "facing right"
#   cast doombot offstage           ...or hidden, until they "drop" in
#   doombot drop at 260             falls in from above, onto the floor at x = 260
#   doombot drop from eye           ...or out of the dark from where his red eye opened
#   player walk to 100 / run to doombot / at 0
#   player face right / face doombot / turn
#   player look angry               a face: normal, closed, squint, angry, shout, hurt, shocked,
#                                   happy, sad, smug
#   player pose cheer               a pose, held until "pose none": cheer, slump, point, shrug,
#                                   laugh, stomp, tremble, crossarms, wave, fist, flex, hips,
#                                   think, facepalm, present, beckon, nod, bow, sit, eat (sat at
#                                   a "table", taking bites of lunch). Whoever is talking
#                                   talks with their hands anyway, in the way the line is said;
#                                   a pose is for the moment that needs more. DoomBot, standing,
#                                   turns to face you, the way Eric drew him.
#   triguy show taunt_surprised     one of his own whole drawings instead - or none
#   player mark anger               over the head: anger, sweat, sparkle, heart, !, ?, steam,
#                                   tears - or none
#   player jump / attack forward / smash up / special down / taunt / block 60 / crouch 40
#   player hold scissors / throw back     an item in hand, and thrown
#   player twirl / roll             a paper turn on the spot, or a spin in the page
#   doombot heat 100 / doombot stocks 3
#
#   camera wide / on doombot / two player doombot / at 0 -900    then any of: zoom 1.3, slow,
#                                   fast, whip, cut
#   wait 30                         frames - sixty is a second
#   shake 40 / sound steam / music stop
#   music story / doombot / battle / menu / victory
#   board at -560 -820 / board clear      where "writes" lines go on the fridge
#   stage bare / stage full         the other drawings off the fridge door, or back on it
#   pile at 520 / pile clear        a heap of taken-down drawings on the floor
#   table at 537 / table clear      a table set for lunch; whoever sits by it gets a stool. It
#                                   is cleared away when the fight starts
#   dark on / dark off              the lights down
#   eye at 0 -900 / eye close       a red eye opening up there in the dark
#   page at 380 -560 / page clear   a new drawing pinned up on the door, with a magnet
#   include player roast            plays the scene called "roast" from the player's own story
#                                   (the finale also includes each player's "thanks")
#   fight                           the scene ends and the fight starts, right where they stand
#   end                             the scene ends, no fight
#
# Every line waits until it is done before the next one starts. End a line with & and the next
# one starts straight away, at the same time.
#
# A chapter list, one line each:
#   title The Headliner                       the story's name, on its map
#   opening [with WHO] [at STAGE]             "== opening", no fight
#   fight WHO [WHO] [at STAGE] [level N] [boss]
#   finale                                    DoomBot on the fridge: "== finale" here, then "== beaten"
#   ending [with WHO] [at STAGE]              "== ending", no fight
# Stages: fridge, plains, rooftops, bigshow, playground, site, kitchen. A fight's scene before is the player's own
# "== vs WHO" if they wrote one, or else that fighter's "== challenger" (two of them: "== team");
# the scene after is the player's "== beat WHO", or their "== won", or that fighter's
# "== defeated" ("== team defeated").
#
# Triguy only ever talks in his catchphrases - and whoever answers him understands exactly what
# he meant and answers it the way you would, so what he said is obvious from the answer. Nobody
# translates him (Eric, 2026-10-15). Circy talks like a normal kid, in memes, VERY energetic,
# with Elim's catchphrases: "duuuuuuuuuude", "GET READY FOR EVIL STUNTS", "okaly dokaly" (Elim;
# Eric's call, 2026-10-19).
