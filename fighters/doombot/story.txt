# DoomBot's story - and the finale every other story ends with.
#
# How a story script reads (the whole language - .ai/story-mode.md has the rest):
#
#   == finale                       a scene, by name, down to the next == heading
#   doombot: HELLO.                 someone says something, in a speech bubble
#   doombot (shout): MINE.          ...said a certain way: shout, whisper, scared, happy, hot, slam
#   circy: [fridge] [=] [doombot]   a picture in the bubble (heart, fridge, doombot, =, ?, !, star,
#                                   magnet, bomb, flame, nofire, square)
#   narrator: One by one...         the narrator, on a strip of paper along the bottom
#   doombot writes: 1. FRIDGE.      burned onto the fridge door with his eye laser (see "board")
#
#   player                          whoever's story it is; anyone else by name (flambe, lugnut,
#                                   edgelord, circy, triguy, doombot)
#   cast player at -400             where someone stands when the scene starts
#   cast doombot offstage           ...or hidden, until they "drop" in
#   doombot drop at 260             falls in from above, onto the stage at x = 260
#   player walk to 100 / run to doombot / at 0
#   player face right / face doombot / turn
#   player look angry               a face: normal, closed, squint, angry, shout, hurt, shocked,
#                                   happy, sad, smug
#   player jump / attack forward / smash up / special down / taunt / block 60 / crouch 40
#   player twirl / roll             a paper turn on the spot, or a spin in the page
#   player hold metre_stick         an item in their hand
#   doombot heat 100 / doombot stocks 4
#
#   camera wide / on doombot / two player doombot / at 0 -900    then any of: zoom 1.3, slow,
#                                   fast, whip, cut
#   wait 30                         frames - sixty is a second
#   shake 40 / sound steam / music stop / music battle / music menu
#   board at -560 -820 / board clear      where "writes" lines go on the fridge
#   include player roast            plays the scene called "roast" from the player's own story
#   fight                           the scene ends and the fight starts, right where they stand
#   end                             the scene ends, no fight
#
# Every line waits until it is done before the next one starts. End a line with & and the next
# one starts straight away, at the same time.

== finale
cast player at -430
cast doombot offstage
music stop
camera on player zoom 1.0 cut
narrator: One by one, the drawings had been coming down.
narrator: And not because of the fights.
player look shocked
camera at 0 -760 zoom 0.7 slow
wait 50
sound stomp
shake 30
wait 30
sound stomp
shake 45
wait 30
sound stomp
shake 60
wait 24
doombot drop at 300
sound special_quake
shake 110
player look normal
doombot face player
camera two player doombot
doombot: GREETINGS, SMALL DRAWING.
doombot: YOU HAVE MET MY... DISTRACTION.
doombot: EVERY NIGHT, WHILE YOU FOUGHT EACH OTHER, I REMOVED ONE DRAWING.
doombot: YOU BLAMED EACH OTHER. NOBODY SUSPECTED...
wait 30
camera on doombot zoom 1.3 fast
doombot: ...THE ROBOT.
board at -620 -820
camera at -120 -680 zoom 0.72
doombot writes: 1. REMOVE DRAWINGS.
doombot writes: 2. REMOVE MAGNETS.
doombot writes: 3. FRIDGE.
camera two player doombot
doombot: THE FRIDGE IS A MACHINE. I AM A MACHINE.
doombot: WHEN THE LAST DRAWING COMES DOWN, THE FRIDGE WILL BE RETURNED TO FACTORY SETTINGS.
doombot: SHINY. CLEAN.
camera on doombot zoom 1.45 fast
doombot (shout): MINE.
camera two player doombot
include player roast
camera on doombot zoom 1.3 fast
sound steam
doombot heat 100
doombot look angry
wait 30
doombot (slam): I. DO. NOT. LOOK. LIKE. THE. FRIDGE.
camera at 1100 -560 zoom 0.9 slow
wait 80
camera on doombot zoom 1.1 slow
wait 70
doombot stocks 4
camera wide
fight
