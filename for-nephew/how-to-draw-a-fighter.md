# Draw your fighter

Draw a character on paper. We put it in a fighting game, where it runs, jumps and fights.

The copy kids actually see is the page on the site, with a picture for every rule:
<https://ecec.dev/draw/guide> (`public/draw/guide/` in the EricClancyEngineeringAndCrafts repo).
It is mirrored as a Claude artifact (<https://claude.ai/artifact/DmdTZS491FFJs28Cj8g5qL>). This
file is the same words without the pictures. **Change one and change all three.** Keep it short and picture-led - the long version
was too wordy for kids, and the reasons behind each rule live in `.ai/art-pipeline.md`, not here.
Paper only: there are no tablet instructions on purpose.

---

## Build it like a person

A head, a body, 2 arms, 2 legs. It can be anything you want, as long as it has those.

- YES: a robot, a shark with legs...
- NOT YET: a car, a blob - no arms or legs to fight with.

## The 4 rules

1. **Outline in black marker.** Not pencil. Colour it in however you like after.
2. **From the side, facing right.** One eye, nose pointing right. The game turns it around for
   you.
3. **Spread out the arms and legs.** Like a big step. Bend the elbows and knees.
4. **Draw it big.** Fill the whole page.

## 4 quick checks

- **Plain paper** - not lined or grid paper.
- **Close up your lines** - no gaps for the colour to leak out of.
- **Hold weapons out** - away from the body, not drawn over it.
- **Give it a neck** - a gap between the head and the body.

## Draw your moves too

On another page, draw your **coolest move** on its own. Draw it 3 times, getting bigger, and it
will move in the game!

Got a pet, a sidekick or a monster friend? Draw that too!

## Take a good photo

Bright light. Phone straight above the paper. No shadows.

## Send it in

**ecec.dev/draw** - add your photos, then answer these:

- **Name** - what are they called?
- **What** - a robot? a wizard?
- **Amazing at** - one thing
- **Terrible at** - one thing (every fighter has one!)
- **Coolest move** - their big special move
- **3 more moves** - a far-away one, one that goes UP, one that protects them

Form not working? Email it to **Eric@ecec.dev**.

## Bonus: draw a stage

A place to fight in, with things to stand on.

- Turn the paper sideways.
- Use light colours, so the fighters stand out.
- Keep the middle empty for the fighting.
