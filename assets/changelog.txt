# What changed in each version, newest first - shown in the game under Options > What's new
# (ChangelogScreen). tools/release/release.sh adds a version here when it is released, from the
# notes it is given. A version starts with "== VERSION | DATE"; each line under it is one change.

== 0.8.2 | 2026-10-19
A new icon: Circy on a page of notepad paper.

== 0.8.1 | 2026-10-18
What's new now shows this version's changes properly.

== 0.8.0 | 2026-10-18
Every stage has its own battle music - punk rock at the Big Show, industrial on the Building Site, funk in the kitchen - and the menu music is calm.
The Big Show's speakers thump to the beat and now and then blast you back.
The Building Site's cranes swing their beams in and out, and you ride along.
Draw a stage of your own and send it in, from Draw your own.
Send us an idea with the Ideas button on the main menu.
Give yourself a name on fighter select; it goes over your head in the fight.
Options, from the gear on the main menu: fullscreen or window, music and sound volume, pad buttons, screen shake, lives, saved names, and What's new.
Computer players in Easy, Medium and Hard.
Team fights colour each fighter in their team's colour.
Crouching drops to one knee, and DoomBot's back arm shows.
The pause menu: Resume, Pick fighters or Main menu. B goes back a screen at a time. Quit asks first.

== 0.7.2 | 2026-10-18
Now installs with a single setup file: download it, run it, and play from the Start menu or the desktop.
Computer players come in Easy, Medium and Hard - pick on the fighter select screen.

== 0.7.1 | 2026-10-17
An Items button on the fighter select screen: Off, Low, Medium or High.

== 0.7.0 | 2026-10-17
Three taunts for every fighter, each with its own line.
Players join in the order they press A.
Flambé and Lugnut sit down to lunch in story mode.
Triguy is white again, coloured in with a white pencil.
DoomBot's eye laser shoots down at an angle.
Grabs reach further.
No mouse pointer anywhere but the drawing screens.

== 0.6.0 | 2026-10-16
Story mode! Every fighter has their own story: an opening, five fights (one of them two against one), DoomBot's big reveal, and an ending.
Up to four players in versus, any of them the computer - every one for themselves, or in teams.
Three big new stages for four players: Playground, Building Site and Kitchen Counter.
Fighters talk with their hands, and celebrate when they win.
Flambé and Lugnut have new faces and new coloured pencil.
In the drawing screen, solid fill colours in with coloured pencil.

== 0.5.0 | 2026-10-12
Items to pick up and use, and a grab button.
A main menu, and a drawing screen to draw your own fighter.
Faces that move, and the Big Show stage.
Triguy's Hat Toss, shields that crack, and limbs that bend the right way.

== 0.4.0 | 2026-10-09
Triguy joins, drawn by Elim.
Lugnut and Flambé redrawn in coloured pencil.
Grabs, ledges to hang on, and shields.

== 0.3.0 | 2026-10-04
New abilities for every fighter, and a new Flambé.
Floatier jumps, and feet that stand on the ground.

== 0.2.0 | 2026-10-03
DoomBot's moves, from Eric's sheet - with heat, and burning.

== 0.1.0 | 2026-10-02
The first download: Circy, EdgeLord, DoomBot, Flambé and Lugnut, stages, menus, a computer player, sound and music.
