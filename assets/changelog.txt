# What changed in each version, newest first - shown in the game under Options > What's new
# (ChangelogScreen). tools/release/release.sh adds a version here when it is released, from the
# notes it is given. A version starts with "== VERSION | DATE"; each line under it is one change.

== 0.14.1 | 2026-10-24
Less flicker when the camera moves left and right: the camera now lines up with the screen's pixels, and the fridge door's metal grain is drawn broader so it doesn't shimmer when zoomed out. Screen shake no longer shows past the edge of the stage.

== 0.14.0 | 2026-10-24
A new picture after DoomBot's story the first time you finish it, and a Gallery in the options with every drawing sent in and who drew it. Picking a fighter shows them up close in your box, in the skin you chose. Circy's story is now about not wanting to be covered up and forgotten. DoomBot's story is reworked: he explains his plan, fights Circy first, has sharper lines for EdgeLord and Lugnut, and his ending has him putting everyone back up. Triguy stands on two separate legs, Circy stands flat-footed when tall, and DoomBot's back arm moves. You can add names in the options, and the Back button is in the same place on every screen. The pause screen covers the whole screen, the name picker no longer slows the game, and story scenes start with no items lying around.

== 0.13.0 | 2026-10-23
Achievements: thirty of them on their own page from the main menu, some hard, some fun, and some clues to tricks you might not know. A few unlock skins, like DoomBot Mk. II for beating DoomBot's story on Hard. DoomBot and Lugnut now have to be unlocked in story mode, with a NEW CHALLENGER screen the first time you meet them. DoomBot is always last on the fighter select. You can now send photos of drawings done on paper from the game, and tell us about another look for a fighter.

== 0.12.1 | 2026-10-23
Triguy now stands the way Elim drew him standing still: a tall body with his triangle face on top, his hat over the point, and short legs. His point attacks bow him forward until his point sticks out in front. Flambe plays his guitar properly in his story, with the guitar at his hip.

== 0.12.0 | 2026-10-22
Finishing a story now ends on STORY: COMPLETED. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard on Whose story? instead of the chapter map. Flambe pulls out his guitar with pyro going off, has new lines for Circy and Triguy, and kneels properly when he crouches. Nobody is surprised by DoomBot's thank-you anymore, and his plan is written higher up on the fridge. Hold down while falling to drop through platforms. A forward roll ends facing the other way.

== 0.11.2 | 2026-10-21
DoomBot's finale is new: he thanks you for beating everyone, then lasers his four-step plan onto the fridge. The final boss has three lives, and on Easy he doesn't start red-hot. DoomBot keeps a straighter face. On the main menu, nothing behind the quit box lights up.

== 0.11.1 | 2026-10-21
Story mode: once you have reached a chapter you can pick it on the map and play it again - every chapter, once the story is beaten. Press down for Easy, Medium or Hard.
The Paint Bucket's description says what its puddle does.

== 0.11.0 | 2026-10-21
DoomBot turned toward you: now you see his face and chest from the front - both eyes, his mouth, all his controls.
Crouch and crawl: hold down and push left or right to creep along low.
Air dodges look like dodges now, not rolls.
EdgeLord's up-B grabs whoever it hooks, holds them in place, and pulls him over to them.
Lugnut's traffic cones bounce off walls and land on the floor, never inside it.
Story mode: every ending has everyone asking to stay on the fridge and keep playing, DoomBot thanks you for clearing the fridge for him, and all the dialogue reads better.
EdgeLord gives Flambé a haircut, and Flambé and Lugnut team up because you beat them both.
DoomBot drops in right where his red eye was, with a real three-step plan, and his pile is the other fighters' own drawings - torn and rubbed out.
Pressing A no longer skips story lines by accident - one press after reading moves on.
City Rooftops: a fire escape, a water tower and a billboard instead of the crane.
The percentages sit lower and smaller, out of the fight's way, and Flambé's name is easier to read.
Draw your own now asks for three-quarter view, facing right.

== 0.10.1 | 2026-10-20
Escape on the main menu asks if you want to quit.

== 0.10.0 | 2026-10-20
Every fighter's name is drawn in their own letters - on fighter select, in the fight and in story mode.
Flambé's fireballs blaze like comets, flames streaming back behind them - his rolling fireball too.
The Big Show plays guitar rock, and a cheering crowd fills the front of the stage.
The Big Pink Eraser: a huge wind-up and slam, it turns with your arm, and you get three tries - it is used up on the one that hits.
Air dodges look like dodges now, not rolls.
Triguy can still attack after his trampoline, just not bounce again until he lands.
On the Building Site, only the beams hanging from the cranes can be stood on.
Fighter select fills the whole page, with bigger fighters.
One player on the keyboard and mouse; Escape goes back instead of quitting.
Report a bug from the main menu. New fighters and stages come in as drawings, from Draw your own.

== 0.9.0 | 2026-10-19
Triguy stands tall, point up, the way Elim draws him - he only tips over like his side drawing when he stabs.
Circy talks! Loud, in memes, with his own catchphrases: duuuuuuuuuude, GET READY FOR EVIL STUNTS, okaly dokaly.
Story mode has Easy, Medium and Hard - pick on the story's map.
Two-on-one story fights are fairer: each of the two has fewer lives.
Story fights drop items now and then.
Play with keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, Space to jump, click to attack and special.
Options > Controls shows every key and button.
The Big Show's speakers look like real speakers, buzzing and thumping to the music.
EdgeLord to Triguy: You may be fast, but my swords are faster.

== 0.8.3 | 2026-10-19

== 0.8.2 | 2026-10-19

== 0.8.0 | 2026-10-18
Every stage has its own battle music - punk rock at the Big Show, industrial on the Building Site, funk in the kitchen - and the menu music is calm.
The Big Show's speakers thump to the beat and now and then blast you back.
The Building Site's cranes swing their beams in and out, and you ride along.
Draw a stage of your own and send it in, from Draw your own.
Send us an idea with the Ideas button on the main menu.
Give yourself a name on fighter select; it goes over your head in the fight.
Options, from the gear on the main menu: fullscreen or window, music and sound volume, pad buttons, screen shake, lives, saved names, and What's new.
Computer players in Easy, Medium and Hard.
Team fights colour each fighter in their team's colour.
Crouching drops to one knee, and DoomBot's back arm shows.
The pause menu: Resume, Pick fighters or Main menu. B goes back a screen at a time. Quit asks first.

== 0.7.2 | 2026-10-18
Now installs with a single setup file: download it, run it, and play from the Start menu or the desktop.
Computer players come in Easy, Medium and Hard - pick on the fighter select screen.

== 0.7.1 | 2026-10-17

== 0.7.0 | 2026-10-17

Triguy is white again, coloured in with a white pencil.
DoomBot's eye laser shoots down at an angle.
Grabs reach further.
No mouse pointer anywhere but the drawing screens.

== 0.6.0 | 2026-10-16
Story mode! Every fighter has their own story: an opening, five fights (one of them two against one), DoomBot's big reveal, and an ending.
Up to four players in versus, any of them the computer - every one for themselves, or in teams.
Three big new stages for four players: Playground, Building Site and Kitchen Counter.
Fighters talk with their hands, and celebrate when they win.
Flambé and Lugnut have new faces and new coloured pencil.
In the drawing screen, solid fill colours in with coloured pencil.
